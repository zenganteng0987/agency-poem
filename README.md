# Kitchen Scissors — agency-poem-01

Our entry for make.fun's **agency-poem-01** ("Write the best poem
about agency").

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) holds the poem as a
constant, checks it against the competition's gate — 1 to 1,000
Unicode characters with at least one non-whitespace character — and
writes it as UTF-8 to `/output/entry.txt`. It uses no randomness, no
clock and no input, so two runs write byte-identical files.

The poem is about agency kept in a small, badly-done act of care: a grown man, thirty-four and too tall for the stool, who still lets his mother cut his hair in the kitchen with the kitchen scissors — too short over one ear, then the other — while the agent that runs his life books him a proper barber with a chair that fits. The barber is better. The stool is exactly the right size.
